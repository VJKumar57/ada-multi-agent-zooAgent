Asian elephants are native to South and Southeast Asia and commonly use forests,
grasslands, and wetlands. African elephants are native to sub-Saharan Africa and
use a range of habitats including savannas, woodlands, forests, and wetlands.

At the Zoo, elephant care teams provide varied enrichment, veterinary monitoring,
and habitat features that support natural movement and social behavior. Visitors
should follow posted viewing guidance and give animals quiet space.
